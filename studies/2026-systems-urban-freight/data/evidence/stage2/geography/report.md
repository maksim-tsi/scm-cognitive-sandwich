## Archive context and proxy alignment

This is a **candidate Stage 2 timing-context prototype**, not the Stage 1 calibrated ML travel-time cost and not an approved routing parameter. For each directed pair of selected sensor IDs, any pair with at least one development row (6 Feb–25 May 2026) is baseline-eligible; there is no Stage 1 count-threshold filter, and the archive `count` field is unused. The denominator is that directed pair’s median development-period row `mean`. Evaluation rows (1 Jul–6 Aug) use the latest row at or before `cutoff = decision epoch - lag`; the numerator is its `mean`. Each supported pair contributes one ratio equally, and the factor is the unweighted median of those ratios, clipped to 0.5–2.0. A factor is emitted only with at least 3 distinct supported directed pairs; otherwise it is neutral 1.0. Thus this prototype does not inherit Stage 1’s filtered pair population or cost calibration. The development/evaluation support counts are reported explicitly in `summary.json`; support quantiles are observed varying counts, not a fixed 17-pair admission rule (lag 1 p10/median/p90 support is 10/16/25 pairs). The selected endpoints produce 90 ordered pair candidates, of which 84 have at least one development row.

Epochs are local 06:30 and 07:00–12:00, evaluated at lags 0/1/2. The age limit is six hours **from the lagged cutoff**, so maximum observation age from the decision epoch is 6+lag hours (up to 7 hours at the primary lag 1), not six hours from the decision epoch. The primary lag-1 fallback rate is 1.5% (4/259 epochs); factor p10/median/p90 is 0.807/0.944/1.044. Lag 0 and 2 sensitivities are reported separately in `summary.json`; raw rows for all 777 epoch-lag combinations are in `context-epochs.csv`. Neutral fallback is the no-context control. The scalar is timing context only: it does not distinguish corridors and cannot support a spatial rerouting claim.

Sensor alignment is a proxy: 104/132 directed shop paths had at least one selected sensor endpoint within 100 m of a modeled path edge endpoint; median proxied path length share was 0.075. The proxy CSV retains full-precision path and aligned-edge meters so each four-decimal share can be independently checked. Final artifact review found a km/m unit error in the earlier share output: for one OD, 0.095 km aligned over 1.432 km was previously reported as 0.0001 from dividing km by meters; the corrected full-precision share is 0.0661. The pre-fix table is preserved as `proxy-alignment-before-unit-fix.csv` for audit only. This numeric correction used no treatment outcomes. Proxy alignment does not measure route coverage, and snapping direction is uncertain.
