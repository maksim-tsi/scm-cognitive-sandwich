# Systems urban-freight study — reproducibility package

**Manuscript:** A Reproducible Research Methodology for LLM-Based Logistics
Decision Support: Lessons from Urban Freight Experiments.
**Authors:** Maksim Ilin; Dmitry Pavlyuk.
**Status:** manuscript, not a journal publication. Package revision: `0.1.0-review`.
The review label describes this export PR. The underlying R1 scientific cohort
was author-approved and frozen on 30 October 2026; its freeze hashes are retained
in `provenance/freeze-records.json`. Original prospective protocol wording is
preserved as a dated source document, not a new request for cohort approval.

## Start here: three reproduction levels

Package installation can require network access. Reproduction itself disables
socket connections and name resolution; it reads neither credentials nor LTX.
`verification.json` and `results.csv` are newly written checks, not new experiments.

## Directory map

- `code/`: original module namespaces, controller, deterministic world and
  reference machinery, gates, solver/checker and provider adapters. Scientific
  functions are preserved; host-specific wiring is sanitized and live worker disabled.
- `data/research/evaluation/`: frozen EVAL30 worlds, references and settings.
- `data/research/eval-op-v2/revision-1/`: frozen R1 assignments, references,
  pair definitions and schedule. Original 16-case precursor remains traceable.
- `data/research/streetview/`: 41-image/12-POI metadata and qualified text only;
  screenshot filenames and hashes are provenance identifiers, not image downloads.
- `data/evidence/`: selected-site register and derived geographical inputs.
  The archived geography summary/report also describe earlier traffic prototypes;
  EVAL30/R1 use the directed distance inputs and stipulated 20 km/h travel proxy,
  not those traffic forecasts or calibrated real delivery times.
- `prompts/`: literal system/schema and exact provider-independent initial
  messages generated by the original public-message function. References and
  nonreleased values are evaluator-only, never model-message inputs.
- `results/`: all 260 assignment records, compact action/evidence steps,
  original gate/evaluation readbacks and source-qualified aggregate analysis.
- `examples/`: original final response texts selected by provider/action/correctness
  strata. They illustrate behavior and are not the population used for statistics.
- `reproduction/`: replay, summary checks, negative tests and dependencies.
- `docs/`: protocols, original scientific reports, visual evidence limits and
  [new collection conditions](docs/new-collection.md).
- `provenance/`: artifact hashes, original-source mapping, code-equivalence
  checks, rights and [qualification record](provenance/qualification.md).

## Keep collections separate

1. EVAL30: 30 assignments each for Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral and D-INT (120).
   Mistral EVAL-29 stopped at the prompt budget: preserve this status separately.
2. Later Luna extension: 30 additional assignments on original EVAL30, not a
   prospectively collected four-model original cohort.
3. R1: 22 assignments per arm (four model arms and D-INT, 110 total).
   There are 108 terminal trajectories and two observed model-format failures.
   The six paired enriched assignments are part of the 16, not extra executions.

Across these separate populations, 257 terminal records admit deterministic
replay; three nonterminal records remain in the package. This aggregate is a
package-integrity count, not a pooled performance metric.

R1 enriched correctness is Qwen 7/16, DeepSeek 6/16, Mistral 4/16, Luna 5/16,
D-INT 16/16. Paired differences are −2/6 for Qwen and 0/6 for other arms;
zero differences can contain two incorrect outcomes. Unsupported proposals
admitted by I2 and rejected by I3 concern filtering fixed proposals, not repaired
model generation or proven delivery safety.

## Replay boundary

Only action arguments, returned scenario information and budget counters are
exported from the world history. Wire requests/responses, reasoning, provider
identifiers, full technical logs and original archives are not exported.
The replay rebuilds the exact original event hash chain from these compact steps.
Dispatcher/lookup responses are re-executed through the original world. Recorded
`plan_route` witnesses are mathematically checked, then replayed unchanged:
re-running a time-limited search is not a deterministic reconstruction of its
selected witness. All original I2/I3 and evaluation dictionaries must match exactly.
This verifies public scientific artifacts, not an independent audit of omitted
provider-wire archives or external ground truth.

## Evidence and external validity

The [original Padua record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22111450) is cited,
not republished. Operational records, order loads, capacities and windows are
explicit synthetic premises. Historical visual descriptions do not authenticate
receivers, loading permissions, real freight entrances or current access.
ALDI customer-access clearance is not a freight restriction; Lidl's candidate
opening is not an authenticated receiving entrance; Komok identity is unresolved.
Visible curbs do not establish legal loading permission. No images are supplied
to experimental models or distributed here; researcher AI-assisted image review
is a separate preparation activity. D-INT correctness is conditional on the
stipulated world and partly shared checking machinery.

Use [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) and the versioned package manifest when citing
this study. No final tag/release is created before author approval.
