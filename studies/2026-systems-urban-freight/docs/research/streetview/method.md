# Remote visual assessment protocol

Recorded before classification, 2026-10-29. The unit of analysis is the 12 selected POIs; each screenshot is separately indexed. The map search coordinate identifies the map target, not the camera, receiving entrance, or currently operating retailer. Coordinate OCR and nearest-neighbour outputs are candidates subject to visual review; near distance alone does not confirm imagery identity. WGS84 ellipsoidal geodesic distances use GeographicLib; coordinate rounding and OCR errors affect apparent precision.

For each POI record exact screenshot references and visible features: storefront/sign identity; roadway and approach; receiving/loading entrance or bay; parking/service access; barriers, steps and canopy; apparent manoeuvring constraints; readable signs; potential curb; panorama date and occlusion. Mark observations as visible, not visible, or unverified, and distinguish inference from observation. Absence of a visible dock is not evidence that delivery is impossible. No dimensions, legal permission, actual vehicle suitability, current operation, delivery windows or receiver authorization are inferred from images.

Physical plausibility categories, assessed from available views collectively:

- **Visually supported:** approach and a specific plausible receiving/loading access are visible and no contradictory physical obstacle is apparent. This is not a vehicle-specific feasibility proof.
- **Conditionally plausible:** some approach or candidate access is visible, but receiving access, manoeuvre or other important conditions are unresolved and no clear material impediment dominates.
- **Potentially constrained:** a visible physical obstacle, geometry or restriction may materially impede access for at least some delivery vehicles; specify the evidence and affected vehicle assumptions. This does not mean impossible.
- **Insufficient visual evidence:** identity/approach coverage is too weak for even a conditional judgment.

Independently describe operational-constraint evidence completeness: **limited** (one or more relevant observable facts but material legal, temporal, physical or receiver conditions unverified) or **insufficient** (no meaningful relevant coverage); **verified** requires authoritative current source evidence for the specific claimed condition and is not conferred by imagery. These labels are not legal or operational certification. A site may have good visual coverage and limited operational evidence.

Record reviewer changes rather than overwriting the original assessment. The four heterogeneous POIs originally queued received a second assistant pass. Independent expert review was not performed and is no longer a PR gate; the limitation remains explicit. Counts use all 12 sites as denominator; image coverage uses all discovered screenshot files as denominator. Derived records are separate from frozen cases. Street View source images remain private and are not published without licensing review.
