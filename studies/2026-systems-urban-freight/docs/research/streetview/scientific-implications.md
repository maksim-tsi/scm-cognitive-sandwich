# Scientific implications of the remote visual audit

The 12-site visual audit adds evidence about selected external physical settings, not observed delivery outcomes. It supports the existence of some store buildings and approach features at the panorama dates; it does not validate present operation, the specific receiving door, loading permission, a particular truck's manoeuvre or the timing assumptions in the frozen synthetic worlds. Approximate road widths and apparent space are not measurements. The selected POIs are a purposive geographic subset, not a representative sample of Padua.

The mapping `poi-scenario-map.json` ties each public site label to its scenario order, field bindings, visual evidence, image references and gaps. As a concrete example, ALDI appears in EVAL-02, 03, 08, 11, 14, 15, 20, 23, 26 and 27. Its pictured 2.10 m customer-entry barrier may matter to a vehicle taller than that at that entrance, but there is no evidence that this is the delivery route. EVAL29 includes Lidl, EUROSPAR, Ali Supermercati and Pam; its modeled cargo and vehicle-aperture checks do not establish that any real receiving doorway, loading point or road is passable. These are candidate external-validity questions, not reasons to relabel EVAL30 outcomes.

A prospective cohort could include source-qualified vehicle dimensions and delivery entrances; constraints on approach/turning, curb use and manoeuvre; permissions and time windows; site-specific unloading method; and conflicting or missing evidence. Only an owner or authoritative source can establish permissions and operational constraints; a visible sign must be checked for applicability, scope and currency. Capture uncertainty, source date, supersession and provenance in each premise. An AI-assisted observation becomes a scenario assumption only after researcher review. All providers must receive the same approved textual facts; none receives the images.

The EVAL30 mechanisms, ground truths, results and raw captures remain immutable. Constructing a new cohort is a proposal rather than a conclusion of efficacy; new cases and references require prospective review and freeze. The Luna arm, once separately authorized, is a later extension of EVAL30 and model/profile differences limit direct attribution. Earlier AI assistance in preparation is documented, without inferring cross-session API leakage.

## Evidence levels and external validity (30 October 2026)

The 41 dated observations in `second-pass-observations.json` distinguish visible features from the classifications in `site-assessments.json`. The `poi-scenario-map.json` maps those observations to scenario IDs and modeled orders/bindings; routing, windows, fleet and handling values are model assumptions, not observations of real delivery operations. Unknown identity, vehicle access, legal permissions, unloading points and current site conditions remain unknown. The Komok target shows Vaillant in the available 2022 views, and association with the named POI is unverified. Neither the assistant second pass nor the user-reported secondary ChatGPT check is independent expert validation. No inter-rater reliability is claimed. Lack of independent validation limits use of these data for real-world conclusions, but is no longer a PR gate. This analysis does not change EVAL30 scenarios, labels, scores, or captures.
