# Padua remote site assessment

Initial assessment 2026-10-29; second assistant visual pass 2026-10-30. Independent expert validation was not performed and is not a PR gate. All 41 local Google Street View screenshots were individually viewed and their search-field coordinates checked against OCR. These identify map targets, not panorama cameras or entrances. All 41 targets match one of the 12 selected POIs; distances are WGS84 ellipsoidal distances from displayed rounded DMS values to selected-site coordinates. Three requested examples match approximately 1.2, 1.6 and 1.5 m. Geographic proximity does not prove the current retailer identity; Komok and Alimentari are unresolved, and the Romanian mini-market identity is not certain. Raw screenshots remain private, and file hashes and panorama dates are in the inventory. Panorama ages span March 2019–September 2026; viewpoints and dates differ even within a POI.

The per-image second-pass observations are in `second-pass-observations.json`. The reproducible record is `site-assessments.json` and the site/scenario mapping is `poi-scenario-map.json`; individual coordinates and supporting filenames are in `screenshot-inventory.json`. The source captures were created by the researcher on 29 October 2026; displayed panorama dates are not that capture date. Camera coordinates are unknown. The visual rubric is in `method.md`. Categories are assistant classifications, not legal certification or researcher adjudication.

Approach is assessable in 11/12 (91.7%) at a qualitative level; a specific receiving/loading access is positively identifiable in 0/12 under the conservative criterion; potential restriction signs or physical limitations are observable at 10/12 (83.3%). All 12 require additional site-specific evidence for operations. An unobserved dock does not mean no delivery is possible. Lidl has a plausible recessed side opening but its function is unverified, and the EUROSPAR truck's destination is not proved. These are not counted as confirmed receiving access.

Examples: ALDI's pictured customer entrance has a 2.10 m sign; it cannot be generalized to a separate service entrance. The small Alimentari candidate has a shallow frontage, canopy and bollards, but current retailer identity and receiving position are unverified. Pam and Alìper show customer parking restrictions; neither establishes freight access. Komok screenshots show a different business, so matching the search coordinates does not settle identity. Site categories use original source labels as retailer type, not a verified measure of store size.

Check the current municipality order and exact route before asserting restrictions.
