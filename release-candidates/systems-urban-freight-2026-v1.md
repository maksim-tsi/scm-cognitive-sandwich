# Systems urban-freight manuscript — reproducibility package

A Reproducible Research Methodology for LLM-Based Logistics Decision Support:
Lessons from Urban Freight Experiments, Maksim Ilin and Dmitry Pavlyuk.
Status: manuscript prepared for Systems, not an accepted/published journal article.

The unchanged study package supports results reproduction and deterministic I2/I3
replay offline. EVAL30, its later Luna extension and EVAL-OP-V2-R1 remain separate.
All 260 assignment records, 257 terminal replays and 87 separate scripted diagnostics
are retained. Model re-execution is a distinct level and identical outputs are not
promised. No original Padua dataset or Street View images are redistributed.
